# Fixes for broken material data in converted scenes, applied at load time
# (src/raytracer/mesh.rs MaterialOverride):
#   FILE  PATTERN  key=value ...
# PATTERN is * or a substring of the material name; later lines win. Keys:
# transmission, ior, specular, thickness, roughness, base=r,g,b.

# Bistro: the conversion marked nearly every material (pavement, bricks, metal) fully
# transmissive, glass with ior 1 (no reflection) and most surfaces with specular 0.
# Reset everything to opaque glTF defaults, then make the real glass glass: lamp
# glass, bottles, signs. Window panes become dark reflective glass and the lit shop
# windows (MASTER_Glass_Exterior, emissive) stay opaque: the buildings have no rooms.
exterior.glb  *                     transmission=0 ior=1.5 specular=1
exterior.glb  Glass                 transmission=1 ior=1.5 base=1,1,1
exterior.glb  MASTER_Glass_Clean    transmission=0 base=0.02,0.02,0.02 roughness=0.05
exterior.glb  MASTER_Glass_Dirty    transmission=0 base=0.04,0.04,0.04 roughness=0.25
exterior.glb  Streetlight_Glass     roughness=0.1
exterior.glb  MASTER_Glass_Exterior transmission=0
exterior-untextured.glb  *                     transmission=0 ior=1.5 specular=1
exterior-untextured.glb  Glass                 transmission=1 ior=1.5 base=1,1,1
exterior-untextured.glb  MASTER_Glass_Clean    transmission=0 base=0.02,0.02,0.02 roughness=0.05
exterior-untextured.glb  MASTER_Glass_Dirty    transmission=0 base=0.04,0.04,0.04 roughness=0.25
exterior-untextured.glb  Streetlight_Glass     roughness=0.1
exterior-untextured.glb  MASTER_Glass_Exterior transmission=0

# Fireplace room: walls, sofa and table marked fully transmissive, ior 1 everywhere.
fireplace_room.glb  *                transmission=0 ior=1.5 specular=1
fireplace_room.glb  :Glass           transmission=1 base=1,1,1 roughness=0.02
fireplace_room.glb  FireplaceGlass   transmission=1 base=1,1,1 roughness=0.05
